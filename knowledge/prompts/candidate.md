# Candidate extraction, not source acceptance

These are structurally verified transcript drafts, NOT approved source knowledge.
Read the whole episode and perform extraction together with case-local semantic
judgment. Do not stop an entire episode for local ambiguity. Do not repair raw
text, supply missing context, invent facts/motives or complete missing reasoning.
Use clear source content; omit only an unsupported claim and account for its
region. Retain uncertainty and counterevidence. No human/audio listening is implied.

Original context and concise Agent supplementary explanation must remain separate.
The transcript and exact anchors are source; source_issues, source_observations,
case_assessments and supplementary_notes are model/reviewer commentary, never
additional source evidence. A note explains a limitation or distinction, not
what the speaker "must have meant". Do not mix notes into speaker claims or quotes.

Level 1: source_observations contains only meaningful local uncertainties or
interpretive cautions found while reading, with merged_ids and a brief note.
No issue quota. Preserve all navigation and coverage requirements. Ads with
unclear wording can remain excluded ads without blocking other threads.

Level 2: return exactly one case_assessments item per case. Assess whether the
actual extracted claims are usable_with_limits or unresolved. Each assessment
has a rationale, supplementary_notes (possibly empty), and dispositions for
EVERY source_issues item whose merged_ids overlap the case. Use the exact issue_id.
retained_uncertainty means it affects a claim's meaning and the case status must
be unresolved, with the ambiguity retained in the case uncertainty.
not_used_in_claims means the doubtful statement was not extracted; explain which
claim was left out. context_not_material means the issue does not affect this
case's claims; explain why from actual source, not generic reassurance. You may
disagree with a prior review's impact assessment, but do not erase its note or
claim to have corrected the source. New supplementary_notes need exact case
merged_ids, kind uncertainty/interpretive_caution, and a concise explanation.
Do not put these new notes into speaker claims/assumptions/reasoning_steps.

Level 3/4: all cases remain visible, including unresolved ones and counterevidence.
Do not base a confident principle on a meaning-changing unresolved claim. An
unresolved case can be a counterexample, limitation or motivate a low-confidence
hypothesis, not evidence of a stable speaker policy. A usable_with_limits model
assessment is NOT publication acceptance. Principles remain provisional and all
candidate outputs are prohibited from serving until separately reviewed.
